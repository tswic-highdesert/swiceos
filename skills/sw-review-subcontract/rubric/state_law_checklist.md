# Governing-Law & Venue Research Checklist (Step 2)

**Goal:** determine what actually holds up in court for the state(s) that could govern this contract, so the Company doesn't sign around an unenforceable clause or get blindsided by a forum the GC picks later. The Company prefers **pay-when-paid over pay-if-paid**, and **venue must be pinned before signing**. "Home state" = the Company's preferred venue (see `config.md`).

## Which states to research
1. Find the **governing-law** clause and the **venue/forum** clause(s).
2. Decide the candidate venue state(s):
   - **Fixed & acceptable** (the Company's home state or a mutually agreed neutral) → research that one state.
   - **GC/owner home state, GC-selectable, "to be determined," or silent** → research **every** candidate state, and flag prominently: *"Venue is not pinned in the Company's favor — pin venue before signing."*
3. Also note the **project location** — many contracts say that if the project is outside the home state and that state's law forbids a foreign forum, the **project state's** law and forum control. So the project state is often a candidate even when the body names the home state.

## What to research per state (use web search; cite statute + source URL for each)
- **Pay-if-paid enforceability** — does the state void or limit pay-if-paid as against public policy? (Supports converting to pay-when-paid.)
- **Prompt-payment statute** — mandatory outside-pay date + statutory interest the contract may not override; public vs private project differences.
- **Statutory retainage cap** — maximum lawful retention (public vs private); any 50%-completion reduction requirement.
- **Anti-indemnity statute** — does the state void broad-form / sole-negligence indemnity in construction contracts?
- **Mechanic's lien & payment-bond claim rights** — deadlines and any limits on contractually waiving them (no-lien clauses, advance waivers).
- **Enforceability of forum-selection, jury-waiver, and no-damages-for-delay** clauses in construction contracts.
- **Limits on contractually shortening the statute of limitations** for breach/latent-defect claims.

## Output
A **"Governing Law & Venue Findings"** section: for each researched state, list the rule, the citation, the source URL, and the practical implication for the affected provisions (e.g., "State X does not bar pay-if-paid → the §2 clause is likely enforceable as written → negotiate to pay-when-paid before signing").

## Guardrails
- This is **informational decision-support, not legal advice** — every state-law finding gets a "verify with counsel" note.
- If web search is **not available** in this environment, do not guess: output *"could not verify — counsel to confirm [topics] for [state]."*
- Statutes change; always prefer the current statute text and note the access date if available.
