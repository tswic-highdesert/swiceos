# Automatic Executive Review Triggers

Any of the following requires review by **Ownership / Executive Management before execution**. In the in-chat triage, group the Red findings under these four buckets in the Executive Escalation block. "The Company" = the party you are protecting (see `config.md`).

## 🔴 Financial Risk
- Pay-if-paid clauses (owner payment as a condition precedent).
- Payment terms exceeding 60 effective days (including "after the GC is paid" stackers).
- Retention exceeding 10%, or retention held to project/owner final acceptance.
- No stored-materials billing.
- Cross-project setoff / withholding on mere belief of breach.
- Contract value exceeding approved bonding capacity.
- Combined long-lead exposure (no deposit + no stored materials + no escalation protection).
- Effective DSO > 75 days or retention release > 90 days past the Company's scope completion.

## 🔴 Schedule Risk
- Liquidated damages (unlimited, flow-down, or uncapped).
- Mandatory acceleration at the Company's expense.
- No-damages-for-delay provisions (including "sole remedy = time extension").
- Owner-controlled completion definitions.
- Unilateral schedule revisions / project-owned float / missing baseline schedule.
- Notice/claim windows under 7 days.

## 🔴 Legal Risk
- Broad-form indemnification or duty to defend on mere allegation.
- Consequential damages exposure (or a carve-out that guts the waiver).
- Unlimited liability / no limitation-of-liability provision.
- Performance guarantee / fitness-for-purpose or design delegation (largely uninsurable).
- Professional liability or pollution/asbestos requirements with no related scope.
- Broad flow-down from an un-provided Prime Contract; "most stringent governs."
- GC-selectable or undetermined venue; jury waiver; shortened limitations period; owner-dispute control over claims.

## 🔴 Operational Risk
- Performance or payment bonds required without compensation.
- Undefined or result-based scope of work.
- Prime Contract not provided (see completeness gate).
- Commissioning/certification ownership without controlled test conditions.
- Open-ended warranties or punch lists; warranty tied to owner occupancy.
- Right to supplement/take over and charge back without cure.
- Site-readiness / environmental preconditions risk shifted to the Company.
